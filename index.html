Mur prem kahini

I might be a mad, or many may call it a blind love. But whatever I did, it was a call from my eternal soul. My love is not trendy, which is inspired by others — it is unique, and it has no limits. It is true that when we love someone our heart replenishes towards them. But in true love the soul is involved.
