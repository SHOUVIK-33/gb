The Feeling

What was never said

There are people who walk into your life so softly that you only notice them by how different the quiet feels after they arrive. This is a page about one of them.

It is not a story with two sides. It is one side — a side that watched, that smiled, that hoped, and that slowly learned to be happy simply because you were happy. No demands, no expectations. Just a feeling that stayed.

One-sided love is a strange kind of honest. You give everything and ask for nothing, and somehow that feels complete on its own.

“Kuch rishte naam nahi maangte — bas dil me jagah maangte hain.”
